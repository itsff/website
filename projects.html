OnlySeriousEmail

Fighting spam with capitalism.

A toll gate for your public inbox. People you trust reach you instantly and for free, while unknown senders pay a small fee — one you set — to land in your inbox. Spam never pays, so it never arrives; genuine messages get through. There’s no app to install and no inbox to migrate, and once a sender pays they’re whitelisted for good. Built for anyone whose address is public — creators, founders, executives, journalists, doctors — who wants to stay reachable without drowning in noise.

Słownik Polonijny

Łelkom, łelkom — the dictionary of Polish-American Ponglish.

A collection of English words and phrases “Polonized” by Poles living abroad — spoken as if they were Polish (think „barbakju dresing”). My father, Zbigniew Roman Frącz, has been gathering them over the years; I built the site to share them with the Polish-American community. Browse entries at random or by letter.
